Weights And Scoring Decisions

How each top-level metric contributes to Overall Tech and what earns the section score.

Overall Tech Formula Equal-weight average

Meta, Schema, URL, H1, Index, Links, and Speed each contribute one available section score. When all seven are measured, each section is worth 14.3% of Overall Tech.

Excluded From Overall Tech Ranking signals

Ranking Keywords and Ranking Potential stay out of Overall Tech. They are used for prioritization and opportunity planning, not grading controllable technical quality.

Scoring Denominator Bad crawls excluded

Rows with no usable Botify evidence are kept in crawl counts but removed from section-score averages so failed crawl records do not behave like real page failures.
